# Blind MCP research round 6, October 3, 2026

Decision: run the loop a sixth time the same day, on the round-5 release, with ten expert personas drafted after surveying every live table and aimed at the families and seams no round had pressed (GAO protest statistics, independent expenditures, lobbying identities, court same-case groups, the classification tables, commenter-to-committee links, bill stages below passage, committee meetings, and the server's own contract as a program would use it). Two held-row defects left open by round 5 were fixed and released first (round 6a: a congress-index row read under an older shape is read once more and then held; a held Senate roll call gains its file columns), so nine personas ran on the round-5 release and the tenth on 6a.

The decision rule for every finding stayed the same as rounds 3 to 5: reproduce it independently (fresh SQL over the pinned Parquet, then the publisher's record), locate the owning layer in the code and in the data, state a publisher limit as a limit, fix a defect at its root with a failing test first, and replay the repair on the built image and on the deployed service.

The run directory is `corpora/mcp-chaos-2026-10-02/round6/` (local, gitignored): persona reports and audits, four root-cause scouts, the no-tools review with the orchestrator's rulings and the owner's eight decisions, nine implementation reports with their receipts, the wheel build, and the release evidence.

## Root causes and repairs

Four scouts traced the issues by layer. A no-tools reviewer then pushed back on fifteen points that were accepted (pin the image's packages before trusting the argument model; unknown, not stale, for a snapshot table; remove a link that cannot be true and do not restate the committee table; check a warning against the filing before writing it; one placeholder predicate) and five that were not, with reasons recorded. The owner decided eight product questions and overrode three recommendations toward the fuller fix: refuse an oversized reply whole and also shrink replies; reconcile removed postings daily now; add a `cleared` stage. A ninth instruction replaced a sample of GAO decision pages with a capture of all of them.

**Acquisition (spicy-docs, the `0.54.0+chaos.a1b91ce28a2a` build of the bills-lane branch).** A mention is no longer a stage step: on an action that carries a code, `introduced` and `committee` fire only on that rung's own codes, a special rule's codes classify nothing, and the text fallback is anchored; before passage only a referral lowers `calendared`. The owner's `cleared` stage reads a bill both chambers have passed in one text, from the completing action (the second chamber passing it as it arrived, agreement to the other chamber's amendment with none of its own, both chambers agreeing to a conference report, or the Library's own "Cleared for White House"); wording that does not establish one text stays `passed_both`. Replayed over 931,774 retained actions, 806 bills change stage (527 `passed_both` to `cleared`, 133 `introduced` to `committee`, 99 `committee` to `calendared`); the 119th's 29 cleared bills are the audit's hand reading exactly; 94.3% of bills that later read presented, law or vetoed read `cleared` just before, and none reads `cleared` and then exchanges amendments. A private law's signing date comes from its coded became-law action. The Federal Register reader keeps the Register's regulations.gov link (docket id, document id, the relayed comments count and when the Register checked it) and four more stated fields (`action`, `correction_of`, `corrections_json`, `significant`); the acquisition policy moves to 1.4 and the raw schema to 1.2. GAO's listing number field is split with a flag where GAO's listing cut the list, and a new reader takes the decided date and the full B-number list from a decision page's caption. The classification-table reader slices the page column where OLRC prints it (reader `laws-uslm-v3`); both OLRC tables key the bare section and state its place in a new `usc_place` (`section`, `note`, `preceding`, `note_preceding`), which takes the cross-table join from 558 wrong-row matches to none; a law states its last Statutes page; a hearing states the committee codes its MODS names; and one predicate reads an FEC name field's placeholder for "none".

**Transforms and publication (spicy-regs).** A posting regulations.gov has withdrawn is no stage evidence: 121 proceedings that existed only through withdrawn postings leave the lifecycle tables, and no anchor rests on one. A daily step re-asks regulations.gov about recently active dockets (at most 1,100 keyed requests) and marks a document it no longer lists (`documents.publisher_status`, `removed_observed_at`); the counts leave a removed document and its comments out. `org_committee_links` drops links that cannot be true (1,417 to candidate committees and leadership PACs), and states FEC's own sponsor for the committee and whether its name agrees. Court same-case groups are rebuilt under rule 2: rows are one case when their captions read the same or were filed the same day with one caption contained in the other; the tier says what the rows are (`per_defendant_dockets`, `duplicate_records`); `match_basis` says why; the representative row is chosen within its court's own id sequence; and the build is a rollup in the repository. A generation records what it read through one read ledger, never its own prior generation as a parent. The citation reads table states its input, and the field-state views distinguish `unread`, `not_stated`, `stated_empty` and `stated`. FEC's two-digit years are typed between FEC's first cycle and the year the file was captured (219,332 cells; three refused as out of range), with the raw text kept; the campaign-finance query family is regenerated as a bounded correction of 14 of its 180 members under a full release receipt. `cfr_sections.part_granule` marks the granule that is a part itself, so a part citation resolves to one row. The Federal Register table keeps the nine new fields on every one of its 1,009,864 documents (245,072 state a regulations.gov docket), re-reads the last 120 days each run so the relayed comment count is not frozen in publication week (a comment period closes a median 32 days after publication), and `fr_docket_links` has one row per document and docket with `link_source` saying which statement carries the pair (792,848 printed, 137,677 from the Register's link, 107,406 both); the rulemaking stages still read the printed statement only. GAO decisions are read from a local capture of 6,614 of GAO's 6,616 decision pages, taken through a proxy on the owner's instruction and recorded as an input of the generation: 6,361 carry the day decided, and the lists GAO's listing cuts fall from 8 to 1 (B-412940, whose page holds no decision text). Both OLRC tables re-derive their bare section key and place from stored columns every run, so the correction did not wait for the publisher's site. A Statutes pinpoint inside a held law's page range resolves to that law and says the match is by range; a page two laws share reads ambiguous with both listed. Each held hearing was read once more for the committee codes its MODS states (184 of 185 state one).

**Declared metadata.** Every table has a `subject` (rulemaking, congress, campaign finance, lobbying, courts, oversight, spending, reference). `lobbyist_id` is described as one registrant's record of a lobbyist, with LDA's own record as the evidence. The GAO text says how GAO's annual statistics count. The SAM text no longer claims a UEI ties a commenter to a registration. Independent expenditures name the cover route that works and say the electronic rows come from daily archives; the billion-dollar lines were checked against five raw filings and are as filed. Status columns set when a row is mapped say so in one shared sentence and point at the view that decides each row. Fifty-odd joins the prose promised are declared and measured. Two release-time lists, neither a gate, name a sentence pinned to data that is no longer live and a status column that holds one value while its text says otherwise; the first would have named the stale GAO sentence within minutes.

**Server.** A tool refuses an argument it does not declare, and states every argument problem in one plain sentence with the arguments it takes. `query_sql` says column names once and returns rows as arrays; a reply past 40,000 characters is refused whole, with how many rows fit and how to ask again, and the citation lookup does the same by page and stops restating what a reply already carries. Lineage is stated as recorded: `inputs_current` is true, false or unknown; an own-family parent is a prior generation, not a lag; a snapshot table lists the snapshot's sources and is never called stale for one; `published_at_basis` says what the timestamp observed. A held field's recorded read is found without a rule set, and a reply defines only the status words it uses. `lookup_agency` refuses a namespace it does not serve and names each parent from RefSpec's rows. `list_sources` groups tables by subject.

**Deployment.** The image installs every runtime package at `uv.lock`'s version and fails its build on any difference; the image this round replaced ran 22 packages the tests never ran.

## Verification boundaries

Local: the full spicy-regs gate at the release commit (generate wrote nothing; ruff, ty, the dictionary check, 5,288 tests), the spicy-docs gate at the wheel's tip (11,867 tests), and every implementer's RED test failing on the prior code first. The exact pushed image replays every control and case file against the public bucket before anything is published (164 controls, 26 new round-6 cases, the round-3, 4 and 5 files); the 26 new cases were also replayed against the live service before the release, where 16 failed, so they can fail. The campaign-finance correction is proved cell by cell (192,886 rows compared; every cell outside the dates, their status and the mapping reason equal to the accepted cell; DuckDB's own parser agrees on every typed cell; the independent expenditure receipt date agrees with the date in FEC's image number on 73,102 rows to the day and never differs by a year), the sealed generation reuses 166 of 180 members byte for byte, and the full receipt is bound inside the image against the projected index with six refusal scenarios each disabling all 72 views.

Deployed: Cloudflare's own record of the Worker's bindings and the container application's image, then the campaign-finance probe (schema of every qualified view, receipts, financial decisions) and the full replay against the live Worker after the rollout. The live replay after the rollout passed: 164 controls, 17 round-3 positives and the round-3 red cases (all green now), the round-4 and round-5 files, 26 round-6 server and dictionary cases, 15 campaign-finance date cases and 11 round-6 data cases, with the FEC reply boundary equal to the receipt; the control set is now 216. The deploy chain's first verification ran while the previous instance still answered (the rollout took about six minutes) and was repeated after it. The probe's last, non-FEC baseline check records rather than fails a declared-schema difference on a table this release does not publish (`members`, republished by another lane with three undeclared columns).

What this round could not check: the Law Revision Counsel's site was under maintenance all day (every page a maintenance notice, the Table III file refused; a proxy receives the same notice), so the classification reader's fix is proved on archived bytes and the stored rows, not on a fresh read; two GAO decision pages never arrived through the proxy; B-412940's list cannot be completed from its page; the removed-postings reconcile has not run (its first run is the 12:40Z schedule, or a named-docket dispatch once the refresh adds its columns).
