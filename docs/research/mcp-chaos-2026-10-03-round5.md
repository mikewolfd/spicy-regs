# Blind MCP research round 5, October 3, 2026

Decision: run the loop a fifth time the same day on the round-4 release, with ten expert personas aimed at the families no round had touched (treaties, nominations, CBO estimates, budget volumes, hearing transcripts, committee meetings, press releases, Senate expenditures, the citation lookup, discovery signals, GAO decisions, Record issues, rulemaking lifecycles against the Unified Agenda, and CFR Titles 41 and 50), audit every answer by another route, let a no-tools review push back on the plan, and then fix every root cause in the layer that owns it rather than describe it. The owner's standing instructions for the round: do not wait on other actors' schedules; use the parsing and reading packages spicy-docs and RefSpec already provide; stay DRY; no interim notes standing in for fixes.

The decision rule for every finding stayed the same as rounds 3 and 4: reproduce it independently (fresh SQL over the pinned Parquet, then the publisher's record), locate the owning layer in the code and in the data, state a publisher limit as a limit, and fix a defect at its root with a failing test first. Two rules were added this round after the review: a selector that nothing holds must refuse rather than answer with an empty "complete" result, and a reader must take the publisher's statement whole rather than the part it first needed.

The run directory is `corpora/mcp-chaos-2026-10-02/round5/` (local, gitignored): persona reports and audits, the four root-cause scouts, the DRY scout, the review, the implementation reports with their receipts, and the release deployment evidence.

Every persona answered; none hit a tool error that was not its own SQL; none timed out; 241 service calls in all. ## Root causes and repairs

Four scouts traced the issues by layer and a fifth audited the round's surfaces for duplication of spicy-docs and RefSpec. A no-tools reviewer then split the release plan that bundled a wheel adoption with text fixes, demanded fixes over caveats in four places, and named decisions that were the owner's. The owner made them (recorded in the run directory): adopt the bills-lane branch build now; compact the citation reply and cap it at 25 default and 100 maximum; publish GAO outcomes as the sentence plus a closed vocabulary; make publisher-absent lists NULL; rename `decision_date` to `released_date`; re-stage every held Congress; add the `calendared` and `passed_both` stage rungs; fix money-bill precision; backfill publication times and show parent lag; release the FNS to FNA succession effective 2026-06-24 with the current successor winning the code assignment; and scope a full comments re-read.

**Acquisition (spicy-docs, released as the `0.54.0+chaos.16c2285ef63e` build of the bills-lane branch).** The stage rule holds once a bill has passed (motions and points of order are never passage; a vitiated passage is undone; "Disagreed to in Senate" reads `failed`), with the Library of Congress passage codes preferred where present: 24 bills move in the 119th, all 24 agreeing with GovInfo's printed versions; 1,330 move in earlier Congresses. The ladder gains `calendared` (655 bills in the 119th) and `passed_both` (51). Money bills: two more fiscal-year spellings and four subcommittee patterns (141 fiscal years filled); simple and concurrent resolutions never carry a kind; `reconciliation` only for a reconciliation measure itself (295 of 173,338 bills change; all 846 appropriations-titled measures keep their kind). The Senate expenditure reader takes every line of a wrapped office label (181 rows). `is_civilian` reads the publisher's `isMilitary` (1,349 rows become false). Publisher-absent lists become NULL across 28 columns, with a `detail_read` column so a consumer can tell a read detail from a list-only row. The GAO listing parser captures the outcome sentence (5,935 of 6,616 decisions) and a new interpretation module maps 295 sentence forms to a closed outcome vocabulary (unmapped stays NULL); `gao_decisions` becomes a table contract with `released_date`, `decision_status`, `outcome` and `outcome_rule`. The GAO product-page reader admits a page that states no topic and reads its unlabeled date, which adds the five Bid Protest Annual Reports FY2021–2025. The MODS reader captures event ids, witnesses and subcommittee names (43 event ids, 21 of them Senate ones Congress.gov lacks; witnesses for 63 of 85 Senate transcripts). A `cbo_feed_items` table publishes every CBO feed item (15,156), so the 361 with no estimate row are an anti-join. The comment extract keeps every attachment the record lists, including restricted ones without a file. The Clerk roll-file lister replaces the retired index page. The rule labels name namespaces rather than host tables; the citation kinds, the CFR identifier grammar, the bill-id parser and the body-text derivation version are exported so consumers stop re-implementing them.

**Transforms and publication (spicy-regs).** The print-citations re-read queue is keyed on the inputs that decide a document's rows (the citation rule set and labels, the text-extraction derivation version, the reader version, the contract columns) instead of the whole spicy-docs code digest, stale documents first, a failing package behind the others: the next run re-reads the 20 reports stuck at rule 004. Each vote chamber is listed on its own, a refused chamber keeps its held rows and the run exits non-zero after publishing the other; an empty new session is not a refusal. `gao_decisions` is shaped through the contract with the prior migrated in place; the GAO repair takes several product pages at once (a prepared candidate adds the five annual reports, 53,074 to 53,079 rows, zero prior rows changed). `rederived_stages` passes published rows as they are, which under the new wheel prevents 6,772 false `stage_changed` events. `document_citation_reads` publishes which held fields were read under which rule. `cbo_feed_items`, the MODS hearing columns and their meeting join (42 of 43 keys resolve), `detail_read` as the read marker, and the RefSpec view at schema 1.2 (the derived current-successors table replaces a verbatim port of RefSpec's walk; the current successor wins the code: FR agency 200 resolves to FNA) are adopted. Eight DRY items delete copies: the GAO feed id, the product URL and id grammar, the chamber map, the media-type split, four bill-id parsers, six API-key resolvers and eight `str_or_none` copies. The output ledger refuses a maintainer path the way the joins record already did.

**Declared metadata.** Fifteen tables lose an overclaim or gain the guidance the personas lacked: the citation coverage states its selection rule; the CFR join note states which titles have a 2026 edition and that parts can be created after an edition; the cfr_sections footer sentence promises section placement only and gives a generation-agnostic predicate; the GAO months-read rule describes year and month scopes; the roll-call summary says a blank Senate count reads 0 and why; hearing and meeting notes say what a NULL event id means and that the Congress.gov route carries no Senate witnesses while MODS does; Senate expenditure guidance names the window per package and the funding-year blocks; treaties point at the MODS referral; CBO coverage names the bill-less items; the FDA petition filings and the Agenda edition label are stated; the comments entry's four stale sentences are restated from the republished export. Sixteen tables' schemas are read from their transforms instead of being declared twice. No interim note remains: every column sentence the wheel fixes ships with it.

**Server.** The citation lookup orders by numeric offset with a unique tie-break, takes a `cite_kind` filter and an `offset`, reports per-kind counts, and distinguishes `not_held`, `not_read` and `read_none_found` (a key nothing holds is refused naming the held spelling; a routed kind the document does not hold is a correct empty answer; a kind nothing emits is refused). `partial` is defined. The per-document and per-kind constants are hoisted once per page from a fixed list (a 100-row reply on the House Administration report falls from 113,534 to 47,041 characters), the default page is 25 and the maximum 100. Replies state `published_at` (when the publisher moved the pointer; the writer lands after this image) and, for derived tables, the parent generation they were built from and whether it is still current. The joins and qualification "basis" texts move into the artifacts the server reads. Every tool description plus its compact schema fits the 2,048-character client budget (the `query_sql` text had been cut at character 1,711 for three personas). `catalog_snapshot_id` is a string, because a JavaScript client rounds an integer above 2^53. The citation kind vocabulary mirrors spicy-docs' thirteen kinds and four parity tests bind the copies the serving image cannot import.

**Vocabulary (RefSpec).** The FNS to FNA succession is adjudicated and released beside batch 1 (27 decided items), and the agency registry view is resealed at schema 1.2 with the derived current-successors table (15 rows), the asserted tables byte-identical.

Local: the full spicy-regs gate on the integration branch (generate wrote nothing; ruff, ty, the dictionary check over 158 tables, 4,986 tests, mkdocs strict, the Worker's tests and dry run), the spicy-docs gate at the wheel's tip (11,624 tests), and RefSpec's fast and slow tiers (6,605 and 446). The built image replays the 112 controls, the round-3 positives, the round-4 cases and the round-5 cases (12 server, 21 dictionary) against the public bucket before the receipt is published; the FEC release receipt binds to the image digest and every refusal scenario disables all 72 views.

Deployed: the same replay against the live Worker after the rollout, with the container's serving version checked. Data-dependent cases stay pending until their runs land: the two Title 41 query cases (the evening cfr run), round 3's laws cases (the overnight laws run), and the new hearing, GAO outcome, votes and stage cases (the runs listed below).

What this round could not check: the live `associatedMeeting` for the 58 transcripts whose Congress.gov hearing record names no meeting (api.congress.gov rate-limited the demo key); GAO's product pages for most decisions (a bot challenge); the publisher's current `duplicateComments` for the four EPA records whose superseded copies produced the stale 53,707 (regulations.gov rate-limited the demo key); and the 108th–118th re-stage before its dispatches run.
