# Qualified AGC, CGS and Environment America readers

The [SpicyDocs source release](https://github.com/mikewolfd/spicy-docs/commit/554dfba52246773a103601d3987d46a7755ef7ae)
adds explicit readers for Associated General Contractors of America (AGC),
Citizens for Global Solutions (CGS), and Environment America. The consumer
adopts them through the existing repeatable scorecard wheel builder, retained
input replay, scoped replacement, and evidence pipeline. The selected
[package receipt](https://github.com/mikewolfd/spicy-regs/blob/main/vendor/spicy_docs-scorecards.json) pins the
installed modules. The
[previous receipt](https://github.com/mikewolfd/spicy-regs/blob/main/vendor/spicy_docs-scorecards-0bee5afcac95.json)
preserves the prior package's evidence.

AGC's qualified Congress editions contain the original House and Senate
annual/lifetime aggregate tables and key-vote indexes. Its publisher page
explicitly embeds the Voxara site, establishing the source relationship.
Publisher member identifiers remain AGC identifiers even when their spelling
resembles Bioguide IDs. Native chamber and district labels, percentages, and
repeated legislative URLs remain unchanged. Member profiles, sponsorships,
individual actions, and item detail pages require separate qualification.

CGS's original report supplies letter grades, recognition graphics, weighted
items, and the complete selected publisher grading guide. It covers a period
crossing Congresses; the reader preserves that period and leaves a single
Congress unspecified. The HTML and PDF item-count statements remain separate
source observations. The reader does not invent individual vote results or a
score reproduction formula.

Environment America's original report supplies complete member scores, action
grids, item narratives, and selected period, award, chart, legend, cohort, and
state-average context. Its conflicting period and award statements remain
literal. The reader retains the publisher's duplicate district labels and
unexplained stars, and normalizes native `NA` to `N/A` while retaining the
original observations. Cohort and state-average rows remain located source
context; they do not become member identities. Overview prose, the contents
page, and state-directory text are outside the emitted scope.

The [independent reviews](reader_independent_reviews_three_20261005.json)
record direct original-source inspection and exact module pins. The
[package adoption](reader_adoption_three_20261005.json) records the installed
replay, unchanged baseline files and dependencies, and consumer checks.
Source and model bodies stay in the private corpus; public evidence contains
hashes and source locators under `hash_only`.

Follow the [qualification ledger](integration_qualifications.json) and
[generated queue](integration_progress.md) for named scopes and remaining
renditions. Package adoption does not establish data publication: each
candidate still needs atomic publication and public-file and hosted MCP
readback before entering the publication ledger. Automatic refresh remains
disabled until its live routes and required private assets are qualified.

After publishing the retained scopes, refresh `scorecard-analysis` against the
accepted source generation and already published congressional inputs. SMART,
CWA, historical source recovery, and every remaining census candidate continue
through the same source qualification and review steps.
