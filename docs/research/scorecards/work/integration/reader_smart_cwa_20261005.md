# SMART and CWA source readers

The [SpicyDocs source revision](https://github.com/mikewolfd/spicy-docs/commit/d79c02aa0077f7423f87c38f5ebbc188e8b06a7d)
registers SMART Transportation Division and Communications Workers of America
readers. The existing wheel builder adds these modules to the previously
qualified consumer package. Its [adoption receipt](reader_adoption_smart_cwa_20261005.json)
records the current check status, exact source scopes, installed replay and
preserved package files. Source and model bodies remain private.

SMART's original committee article supplies a collective assessment of its
printed member cohort. That assessment remains publisher wording; it does not
become an inferred official roll or aggregate score. The archived House and
Senate PDFs retain their independent legends, scores, vacancies, annotations
and action grids. The House cosponsorship item remains a bill action. The
[inventory supplement](publisher_inventory_extension_smart_20261005.json)
keeps SMART separate from the historical United Transportation Union lead.

CWA's named rendition includes the complete descending member lists and
sparse key-vote grids. It retains annual and lifetime values, bare percent
symbols, blank scores, source party labels and grid contexts. The reader
refuses unconsumed header and pagination notes or context changes between
pages. Different list, profile and filtered renderings remain separate source
observations.

The [independent reviews](reader_independent_reviews_smart_cwa_20261005.json)
bind both readers to their original source packets and closed drift findings.
The shared PDF primitive, unrelated runtime files and dependency declarations
remain unchanged. Historical evidence pins remain available.

See the [qualification ledger](integration_qualifications.json) and
[generated progress](integration_progress.md) for current scopes. Each source
generation needs atomic publication and public-file and MCP readback before
entering the publication ledger. Live refresh requires its own route and host
configuration checks. Member and item resolution use existing published
congressional inputs downstream.
