# Drum Major Institute source readers

The [integrated SpicyDocs revision](https://github.com/mikewolfd/spicy-docs/commit/f25cacdb768e229a8c45622701110203720043d7)
registers the original 2005 and 2007 Drum Major Institute scorecards. The
[package adoption receipt](reader_adoption_dmi_20261005.json) binds the frozen
reader source, repeated wheel build, installed retained replays and consumer
checks. The scorecard overlay preserves unrelated package files and dependency
metadata while the source merge includes the separately maintained bill and
GAO changes.

Both readers retain the publisher's percentages, grades, member identities,
item descriptions, action symbols, exclusions and methodology wording. The
2005 reader follows the visible printed Senate content where opaque overlays
hide older text. The 2007 reader follows the original thumbs legend and keeps
its incomplete-score rule separate from the earlier edition. Conflicting
publisher dates and descriptions remain source facts.

The [independent reviews](reader_independent_reviews_dmi_20261005.json) approve
only these named original renditions. Their source and extraction bodies stay
in the private corpus. Institutional and party summaries remain source context;
typed aggregate-subject tables and independent score reproduction remain
outside this reader's support.

See the [qualification ledger](integration_qualifications.json) and
[generated progress](integration_progress.md) for current coverage. The
registry remains disabled until its live route and refresh policy qualify.
Atomic data publication, public-file and MCP readback, and downstream identity
resolution remain separate acceptance steps. Historical evidence pins remain
available.
