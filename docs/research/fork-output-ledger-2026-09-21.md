- **Pins.** A row records `qualified at <pin> (<date>)` for the generation its audit covered, not a claim that the pin is live; `scripts/check_ledger_pins.py` reads the public index and reports rows whose live generation differs. - **Names.** A generation is named by the first 8 hex digits of its `artifactDigest`; a table digest is labelled "table"; units are named (rows, distinct keys, pairs).
